CENTRAL BOARD OF DIRECT TAXES
Circular No. 12 of 2022 / F. No. 370142/27/2022-TPL
Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue
New Delhi, Dated 13th September, 2022

SUBJECT: Guidelines under sub-section (2) of Section 194R of the Income-tax Act, 1961 – Reg.

1. Finance Act, 2022 inserted a new section 194R in the Income-tax Act, 1961 with effect from 1st July, 2022. It mandates that any person responsible for providing to a resident any benefit or perquisite, whether convertible into money or not, arising from business or the exercise of a profession by such resident, shall, before providing such benefit or perquisite, ensure that tax has been deducted in respect of such benefit or perquisite at the rate of 10% of the value or aggregate of value of such benefit or perquisite.

2. Threshold Limit:
No deduction is required if the aggregate value of such benefit or perquisite provided or likely to be provided to a resident during the financial year does not exceed twenty thousand rupees (INR 20,000).

3. Business Conferences and Dealer Incentives:
Where a company sponsors free air tickets, hotel accommodations, or dealer incentives to distributors/dealers upon achieving sales targets, the whole expense represents a taxable perquisite under Section 194R requiring mandatory 10% TDS withholding.
