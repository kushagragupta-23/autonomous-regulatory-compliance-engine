Notification No. 31/2026: Strict TDS Enforcement on Immovable Property Transfers under Section 194-IA

Published Date: 25 September 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBDT (Income Tax)
Statute: Income Tax
Document Type: Notification
Urgency: Medium

Summary:
Clarifies 1% TDS deduction requirement on gross consideration including car parking, club membership, and maintenance deposits for property purchases exceeding INR 50 Lakhs in residential and commercial developments.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
