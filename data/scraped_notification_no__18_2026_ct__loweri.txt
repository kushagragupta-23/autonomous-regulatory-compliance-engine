Notification No. 18/2026-CT: Lowering Mandatory E-Invoicing Threshold to INR 5 Crore

Published Date: 28 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBIC (GST)
Statute: GST
Document Type: Notification
Urgency: High

Summary:
CBIC notifies all registered taxpayers with aggregate annual turnover exceeding INR 5 Crore in any preceding financial year to mandatorily issue electronic invoices with IRN and signed QR code under Rule 48(4) of CGST Rules with effect from 1st December 2026.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
