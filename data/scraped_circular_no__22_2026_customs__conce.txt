Circular No. 22/2026-Customs: Concessional Tariffs under Project Import Regulations for Solar & Wind Farms

Published Date: 12 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBIC (Customs)
Statute: Customs
Document Type: Circular
Urgency: Medium

Summary:
Ministry clarifies classification of imported photovoltaic solar cells, balance of plant equipment, and wind turbine assemblies under concessional basic customs duty rates for renewable power generation projects under Heading 9801.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
