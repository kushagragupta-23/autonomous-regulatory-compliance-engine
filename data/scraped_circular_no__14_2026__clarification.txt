Circular No. 14/2026: Clarifications on TDS Withholding under Section 194R on Business Incentives

Published Date: 02 November 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBDT (Income Tax)
Statute: Income Tax
Document Type: Circular
Urgency: Medium

Summary:
CBDT issues comprehensive guidelines clarifying valuation of non-monetary perquisites and vendor performance incentives provided in IT/ITES conventions. Clarifies mandatory 10% TDS withholding deduction under Section 194R on aggregate benefits exceeding INR 20,000 per financial year for software companies and service providers.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
