Circular No. 19/2026: Revised Transfer Pricing Safe Harbour Rules for IT & KPO Services

Published Date: 24 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBDT (International Tax)
Statute: Income Tax
Document Type: Circular
Urgency: Medium

Summary:
CBDT updates Rule 10TD Safe Harbour operating profit margins for provision of software development services and knowledge process outsourcing to foreign associated enterprises, capping standard margin requirements at 17% for transactions under INR 200 Crore.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
