Notification No. 03/2026-MCA: Mandatory Reporting of Significant Beneficial Ownership (SBO)

Published Date: 01 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: Ministry of Corporate Affairs
Statute: Corporate Tax
Document Type: Notification
Urgency: High

Summary:
MCA mandates immediate disclosure of ultimate beneficial ownership stakes exceeding 10% in private and public companies under Section 89 and Section 90 of Companies Act 2013, with steep penalties for corporate non-disclosure.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
