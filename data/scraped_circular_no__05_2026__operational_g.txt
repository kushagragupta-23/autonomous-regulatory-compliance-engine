Circular No. 05/2026: Operational Guidelines for Concessional Tax Regime under Section 115BAC

Published Date: 08 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBDT (Income Tax)
Statute: Income Tax
Document Type: Circular
Urgency: Low

Summary:
Operational guidance for individual professionals and HNI taxpayers exercising option under Section 115BAC(6). Outlines rules for rebate under Section 87A and unabsorbed depreciation carry-forward limitations.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
