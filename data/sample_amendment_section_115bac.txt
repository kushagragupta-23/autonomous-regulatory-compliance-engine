MINISTRY OF FINANCE (DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE)
Finance Act, 2024 / CBDT Circular No. 04/2024
New Delhi, Dated 1st August, 2024

SUBJECT: Clarification regarding Amendments to Section 115BAC – Revised Concessional Tax Regime

1. The revised tax rates under sub-section (1A) of section 115BAC of the Income-tax Act, 1961 shall be the default tax regime for assessment year 2025-26 onwards.
2. Standard Deduction:
For salaried individual employees opting for or defaulted into the Section 115BAC regime, the standard deduction is enhanced from INR 50,000 to INR 75,000.
3. Family Pension Deduction:
Deduction for family pension under clause (iia) of section 57 is enhanced from INR 15,000 to INR 25,000.
Taxpayers desiring to opt out of the default regime to claim Chapter VI-A deductions must exercise the option under Form 10-IEA prior to the due date of return filing.
