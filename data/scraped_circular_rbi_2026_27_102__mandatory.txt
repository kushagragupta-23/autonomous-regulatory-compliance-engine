Circular RBI/2026-27/102: Mandatory Registration & Capital Adequacy for Cross-Border Payment Aggregators (PA-CB)

Published Date: 15 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: RBI (Banking & FEMA)
Statute: FEMA & Banking Regulations
Document Type: Order
Urgency: High

Summary:
Reserve Bank of India mandates minimum net worth of INR 25 Crore for entities processing cross-border inward and outward payment transactions for digital goods and software exports under Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA 1999).

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
