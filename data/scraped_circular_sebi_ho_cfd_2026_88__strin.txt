Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/2026/88: Stringent Disclosure Norms for Material Related Party Transactions (RPT) and BRSR Core ESG

Published Date: 10 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: SEBI (Capital Markets)
Statute: Securities Regulations
Document Type: Circular
Urgency: Medium

Summary:
SEBI issues comprehensive amendments to Regulation 23 of LODR Regulations, requiring prior audit committee approval and shareholder sign-off for brand licensing and royalty payments exceeding 5% of consolidated turnover.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
