Notification No. 44/2026-DGFT: Extension of EPCG Export Obligation & Advance Authorisation Amnesty Scheme

Published Date: 18 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: DGFT (Foreign Trade)
Statute: Foreign Trade Policy
Document Type: Notification
Urgency: High

Summary:
Directorate General of Foreign Trade grants one-time relief and extension for redemption of Advance Authorisation and Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG) authorisations, allowing zero-customs duty regularisation upon payment of capped composition fees.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
