Instruction No. 08/2026-GST: Standard Operating Procedure for Scrutiny of Input Tax Credit (ITC)

Published Date: 20 October 2026
Regulatory Authority: CBIC (GST)
Statute: GST
Document Type: Instruction
Urgency: High

Summary:
Guidelines issued to field formations for verification of Input Tax Credit availed under Section 16(2)(aa) versus auto-populated GSTR-2B. Imposes strict audit action and interest recovery under Section 50 for mismatched tax invoices from delinquent suppliers.

Statutory Directive:
Mandatory regulatory compliance required across all covered enterprise entities under Indian jurisdiction.
